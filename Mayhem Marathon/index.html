How to play

Keyboard + mouse / trackpad

WASD run
Space jump
Shift or C dive (a forward lunge)
F or hold left click: grab / steal a tail
Mouse (click the game first) or ←→ QE: turn the camera, ↑↓ tilt
R back to the last checkpoint (races)
Esc pause (solo) / menu
Spectating: click or Space to watch someone else

Touch

Left stick to run, drag the right side to look, buttons to jump, dive and grab.

The show

Races: reach the finish before the spots run out.

Survival: stay on until the time is up.

Team rounds: the team with the lowest score is out. In Tail Chase, have a tail when time runs out.

Final: the winner takes the crown!

With 2-4 friends a show can be scored by points, so nobody is knocked out. Every round earns kudos and fame for new looks.

Settings
