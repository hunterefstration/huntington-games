How to play

←→ or AD move
Space, W, ↑ or Z jump (hold to jump higher)
Shift or X dash (once you have found it)
Esc or P pause · R back to the last lantern
Gamepads and touch screens work too.

Touch a lantern to light it: it is where you come back to. Collect sparks, find the three hidden star moths in every level, and reach the gate. Shrines at the end of each world teach you something new.
