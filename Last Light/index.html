How to play

←→ or AD move
Space, W, ↑ or Z jump (hold to jump higher)
Shift or X dash (once you have found it)
↓ or S (hold) drop through a thin ledge
Esc or P pause · R back to the last lantern
Gamepads and touch screens work too.

Touch a lantern to light it: it is where you come back to. Collect sparks, find the three hidden stars in every level, talk to the people you meet (each knows part of the story), and reach the gate. Shrines at the end of each world teach you something new, and a guardian waits: when it shows !, stomp it or dash into it.
