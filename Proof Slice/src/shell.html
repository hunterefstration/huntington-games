The realistic look, tested small: light worked out in Blender, real models, animated figures. It has to look right and hold 30 FPS on a Chromebook before anything bigger is built.

Yard: WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, right button aim, R reload, Shift run. Range: WASD drive, mouse aims the gun (left, right, up and down), click fires, hold the right button or Shift for the gun sight. Esc: back here. F3: numbers. For a fair speed test, go full screen first.
