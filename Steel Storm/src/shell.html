How to play

Goal

Team Battle: first team to 15 kills. Conquest: hold the 3 flags. The team that holds more flags drains the other team's tickets. Each death also costs a ticket.

Progress

Every match earns credits and research points. Pick a vehicle to research in the Research screen, then buy it with credits and add it to your lineup in the Garage.

Party

Press Create party, share the 6-digit code. Friends press Join. The leader picks the mode and presses PLAY; bots fill the rest.
