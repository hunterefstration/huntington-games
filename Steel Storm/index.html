How to play

Modes

Team Deathmatch: first team to the kill limit. Conquest: hold the 3 points; the team holding more drains the other team's tickets. Capture the Flag: drive (or fly a helicopter) over the enemy flag and bring it to your own base while your flag is home. Free-for-all: everyone against everyone.

Progress

Every match earns credits and research points. Research vehicles in 15 branches (tiers I-V), buy them, then research modules for each vehicle you own (engine, armour, guns, ammo, stabiliser / countermeasures / radar). Battle rating keeps you with vehicles like yours.

Multiplayer

Open Multiplayer: join an open game from the list, type a friend's 6-digit code, or Host one with your own options. In the party lobby pick a team and press READY; the host starts, bots fill the empty slots. Friends can join a running battle, and a dropped player gets their vehicle and score back.

Respawning

When you are destroyed, the vehicle you picked before the battle is ready first. Pick another one from your lineup (keys 1-5) and press DEPLOY or Space.

Steel Storm 2.3: what's new

Training

New Training button in the menu: three hands-on lessons where you do each step yourself. Basic training (drive, aim, fire, destroy, capture, respawn), Wings and rotors (planes and helicopters) and Your garage (research, buying, modules, the lineup and multiplayer). The practice battles never count for your stats or the leaderboards.

Steel Storm 2.2

New menus

Everything is one click away: Home, Battle, Multiplayer, Garage and Research down the side. Your vehicle is shown large with clear bars for hit points, armour, speed and firepower, its weapons and its role. Works with the mouse alone and on small screens.

Showcase footage

Behind the menu your selected vehicle fights a small live battle, played the way an expert would play it: tanks fire on the move, fighters lock missiles, bombers drop on groups, helicopters pop up from cover, warships turn broadside. Pick another vehicle and the footage switches. Too heavy for your computer? Settings turns it off, and it switches itself off if the menu gets slow.

Multiplayer

A real Multiplayer screen: a list of open games to join, a join-by-code box, and hosting with clear options (mode, map size, length, bots, friendly fire, weather, public or friends only). The party lobby updates live: everyone's vehicle and readiness, teams, host tools (kick, move, shuffle, balance, start), chat, the room code with a copy button, and the connection state (connecting, reconnecting, the host left).

Respawning

After you are destroyed, the vehicle you picked before the battle is selected again. Switch with a click or keys 1-5, deploy with one click or Space.

Your save

Everything you had is kept.

Steel Storm 2.1

Runs smoother (Medium graphics about a third faster, automatic resolution). A full rebalance: each tier is a clear step up and every class has its job. Guided missiles that lead and lock (hold your aim half a second for the red LOCK). Bots pick their gear by skill. Tanks climb hills properly.

Steel Storm 2.0

Party lobby with teams, ready-up and host options; smoother netcode with reconnects and host migration; Capture the Flag and Free-for-all; real sun, shadows, weather and textured terrain; 69 vehicles in 15 branches up to tier V with 5 modules each; guided missiles, flares, torpedoes and diving submarines.
