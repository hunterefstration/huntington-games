How to play

Modes

Team Deathmatch: first team to the kill limit. Conquest: hold the 3 points; the team holding more drains the other team's tickets. Capture the Flag: drive (or fly a helicopter) over the enemy flag and bring it to your own base while your flag is home. Free-for-all: everyone against everyone.

Progress

Every match earns credits and research points. Research vehicles in 15 branches (tiers I-V), buy them, then research modules for each vehicle you own (engine, armour, guns, ammo, stabiliser / countermeasures / radar). Battle rating keeps you with vehicles like yours.

Party

Create party and share the 6-digit code (Copy). In the lobby click a team to switch, press READY. The host sets the options and starts; bots fill the empty slots. Friends can join a running battle, and a dropped player gets their vehicle and score back.

Steel Storm 2.0: what's new

Multiplayer

A real party lobby with teams, ready-up, lineups, host options (mode, map size, length, bots, friendly fire, weather), chat and a copyable room code. Smoother netcode: the host decides every hit, other vehicles glide with jitter-aware interpolation, a ping meter, and Wi-Fi drops survive (reconnect, rejoin with your score, host migration). New modes: Capture the Flag and Free-for-all, a scoreboard with ping, and an MVP on the results.

Looks

A real sun with soft shadows, sky and fog that match four weather presets (clear noon, golden evening, overcast rain, night with flares), tone mapping, flashes that light up the ground, fresnel water, and textured terrain, roads, airstrips and vehicles (camouflage, panel lines, rivets, numbers, weathering). All drawn in code.

Vehicles

39 new vehicles (69 in all) in 15 branches up to tier V: light, medium and heavy tanks, tank destroyers, anti-air, artillery; fighters, attackers, bombers, helicopters; patrol boats, destroyers, cruisers, battleships and submarines. Each has its own model. Every vehicle has 5 modules to research. Guided missiles, flares, torpedoes and diving submarines.
