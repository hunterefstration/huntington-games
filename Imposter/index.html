How to play

The old lighthouse must never go dark. Most of you are Keepers, lantern-robots who tend the lamps. Hidden among you are one to three Shades.

Keepers do chores around the lighthouse (trim wicks, fill oil, copy the logbook...). Fill the lamp bar, or cast out every Shade, to win.

Shades blend in, snuff out Keepers, slip through trapdoors and cause mischief. They win when they equal the Keepers, or when the boiler bursts. Their chores are fake.

WASD / arrows move. E or Space use (chores, the bell, valves). R call out a fallen keeper. Shades: Q snuff, V trapdoor, X mischief. M map. Touch: use the big buttons and drag the left side to walk.

Mischief: snuff the lamps and everyone sees only a little way (relight them in the Lamp Room). Overload the boiler: two Keepers must hold the two valves at once before the timer ends.

Gatherings: find a fallen keeper (R) or ring the bell in the middle of the lighthouse. Talk, then vote. A tie or a held lamp casts out no one. Whoever is cast out into the fog becomes a spirit: spirits still finish chores and whisper, but only to other spirits.
