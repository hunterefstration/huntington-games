How to play

Crewmates do tasks around the ship. Fill the task bar, or vote out every Imposter, to win.

Imposters blend in, kill crewmates and sabotage. They win when they equal the crew, or when the reactor melts down.

WASD / arrows move. E or Space use (tasks, emergency button, fix panels). R report a body. Imposters: Q kill, V vent, X sabotage. M map. Touch: use the big buttons and drag the left side to walk.

Sabotage: lights shrink crew vision (fix at the Electrical panel). Reactor: two players must hold the two panels at once before the timer ends.

Meetings: find a body (R) or press the table button. Talk, then vote. Ties or skips eject nobody. Dead players become ghosts: they still finish tasks and chat, but only with other ghosts.
