What's New in v2.0

100 Levels!

  • 50 brand-new hand-built levels (51-100) after the original 50.
  • 7 new mechanics, introduced one at a time: moving platforms, bounce pads, crumbling blocks, conveyor belts, rotating saws, keys & doors and gravity-flip zones - then mixed together for the final stretch.

Level Editor & Share Codes

  • Build your own levels with every tile, hazard and mechanic, set the start and goal, and test-play instantly.
  • Save as many levels as you like in My Levels.
  • Every level has a short share code - send it to friends, or paste theirs to play their level.

Quality of life

  • Your progress saves: Continue and Level Select on the main menu.
  • Pause any time with Esc (or the II button), quick retry with Enter or R.
  • Same game speed on every screen, and smoother on Chromebooks.
